ABOUT FOLLE

Character matters more than the label.

Folle is a Pakistan-based fragrance business offering perfumes inspired by well-known designer scent profiles.

The collection spans fresh, floral, woody, oriental, seasonal, office and occasion-focused fragrances.

The brand positions itself around long-lasting, premium-quality and pocket-friendly fragrances while making luxury-inspired scent experiences more accessible.
